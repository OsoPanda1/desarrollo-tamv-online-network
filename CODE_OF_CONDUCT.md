# Código de conducta / Code of Conduct

## Our commitment / Compromiso

TAMV Online Network aims to be a respectful, accessible and evidence-oriented environment for developers, researchers, students, artists and territorial communities. Participation is open to people regardless of experience level, nationality, language, identity, background or viewpoint. Disagreement about technical and academic claims is welcome; harassment is not.

TAMV Online Network busca ser un entorno respetuoso, accesible y orientado a la evidencia para desarrolladores, investigadores, estudiantes, artistas y comunidades territoriales. Se permite disentir sobre argumentos técnicos y académicos; no se permite acosar a otras personas.

## Expected behaviour / Conducta esperada

- Use respectful language and critique work, claims and methods rather than attacking people.
- Explain disagreement with reasons, sources, reproducible examples or clearly labelled uncertainty.
- Accept review, corrections and decisions without retaliation or personal pressure.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, intellectual property and the context of territorial knowledge.
- Make collaboration accessible: explain acronyms, provide reproducible steps and consider language and disability access.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when they affect a technical, editorial, governance or funding decision.

## Unacceptable behaviour / Conducta inaceptable

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, stalking, intimidation, deliberate disruption, retaliatory behaviour, impersonation, publishing private data without consent, and knowingly fabricating review/endorsement evidence are unacceptable. Spam, artificial engagement and deceptive claims about adoption or validation are also out of scope.

## Scope / Alcance

This code applies to repository issues and pull requests, code review, project spaces and events explicitly operated by maintainers. A third-party forum or provider may have its own rules; the scope of this document does not imply control over that service.

## Reporting and enforcement / Reportes y aplicación

Report a concern privately to the repository maintainers through a verified contact channel shown on the official repository profile. For security vulnerabilities, use GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting path described in SECURITY.md rather than a public issue. Do not publish sensitive material to prove a complaint.

Maintainers should review reports fairly, preserve confidentiality where possible, avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate a reasoned action. Available actions can include a private warning, content removal, temporary participation limits, or exclusion from project spaces. The severity and context of the situation matter; this policy does not guarantee a fixed response time or outcome.

## Research integrity / Integridad de investigación

No contributor should be pressured to portray a proposal as a finding, a simulation as an observed outcome, a template as a real peer-review decision, or a repository record as independent accreditation. Correcting a factual error or asking for evidence must not be treated as hostility.
